# Contact Quality Rating Policy (v3.1)

Scores use a 0.000–1.000 scale. A metric's rating is determined solely by its
current-cycle score:

| Rating | Score range |
|---|---|
| Exemplary | score >= 0.900 |
| Meets Standard | 0.750 <= score < 0.900 |
| Watchlist | 0.600 <= score < 0.750 |
| Critical | score < 0.600 |

## Priority actions

Every metric rated **Watchlist** or **Critical** must appear in the page's
Priority Actions section. They are listed from the lowest score to the highest
(ties broken alphabetically by metric name). Each bullet uses this format:

`- <Metric Name> — <score to three decimals> (<rating>)`

Metrics rated **Meets Standard** or **Exemplary** must not appear in that
section.
